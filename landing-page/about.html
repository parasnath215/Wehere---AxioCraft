Our Story

Connecting Minds.
Healing Together.

Wehere was born from a simple belief: no one should have to struggle silently. Meet the team working to build a world where people help people.

K

Kanishk Kashyap

Director / CEO / Founder

There was a time in my life when I felt completely alone.

I was going through a difficult period, facing things that I didn't always know how to deal with. And during those moments, I realized something that changed the way I looked at people and at the world around me. Sometimes, what a person needs is simply someone who is there.

Someone to listen. Someone to understand. Someone to remind them that they are not alone.

As I went through my own difficult times, I started observing the world differently. I looked at the people around me and at what was happening in society, and I realized that something needed to change.

My life at sea also gave me a completely different perspective. Living and working far from home, spending long periods surrounded by the ocean, and meeting people from different countries and cultures showed me that, despite how different our lives may look, the struggles we carry inside can be remarkably similar.

The world is moving faster than ever, but somehow, people are becoming more disconnected from each other. People are struggling silently. People are carrying pain that nobody sees. People need support, understanding and genuine human connection.

And I started believing that maybe the solution doesn't always have to come from somewhere outside us. Maybe we need to start helping each other. I believe every person has the ability to make someone else's life a little better. A conversation, a kind word, a few minutes of listening, or simply being present for someone can mean more than we realize.

That belief became the foundation of WeHere. I created WeHere with a simple dream: To build a world where people help people.

"Sometimes, peace begins when you know someone is there."

N

Nailah Istifa

Co-founder & COO

One night, while living alone in a city far from my family, I felt like I’d lost myself.

I’ve always been an overthinker. Doomscrolling fed it, and with no friends or family nearby, I had no one to say it out loud to. That night I realized the problem wasn’t only the world outside. It was being alone with my thoughts.

Most of what weighs on us gets lighter the moment someone says, “I feel that too.”

So we built WeHere: a community where people facing similar struggles find each other, talk openly, and feel understood. It isn’t medical treatment. It’s human connection, and a reminder that you’re not alone.

"No one should have to get through a hard night by themselves."

S

Srishti Gautam

Additional CMO

As a Relationship & Partnerships Manager at an Ed-Tech organisation, I spend a lot of time understanding people—their aspirations, challenges, and the pressures they carry silently.

Through these interactions, I realised how many people struggle with stress, anxiety, loneliness, and emotional overwhelm, yet often don’t know where to turn or feel comfortable asking for help. This made me realise that while we invest heavily in education and professional growth, we often overlook the emotional well-being that helps us truly thrive.

That realisation inspired me to create a mental health app that feels like a safe, accessible, and judgment-free space—where people can pause, express themselves, understand their emotions, and find the right support.

"At its heart is a simple belief: no one should have to struggle silently when support can be made easier to reach."
