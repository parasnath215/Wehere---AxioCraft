WEHERE SAFETY, SELF-HARM & CRISIS RESPONSE POLICY

Effective Date: 10th October 2026

Last Updated: 7th October 2026

1. Purpose

This Policy explains WeHere's approach to self-harm, suicide-related content, serious emotional distress and crisis situations.

WeHere is a peer-support platform and is not an emergency service, hospital, medical provider, psychiatric provider or substitute for professional care.

2. Permitted Discussion

Users may discuss:

loneliness;

grief;

emotional distress;

relationship difficulties;

personal struggles;

recovery experiences;

difficult life circumstances; and

seeking peer support.

3. Prohibited Conduct

Users must not:

encourage suicide;

encourage self-harm;

provide dangerous instructions;

glorify self-harm or suicide;

pressure another person to self-harm;

organise self-harm challenges;

exploit a person in crisis;

deliberately intensify a crisis; or

use self-harm threats to manipulate another

user.

4. Crisis Disclosures

Where WeHere becomes aware of a credible safety concern through reporting or available safety systems, it may:

review content;

restrict harmful content;

restrict an abusive account;

provide appropriate safety information;

escalate internally;

preserve relevant information where lawful; or

contact appropriate authorities where legally required or reasonably necessary.

5. Immediate Danger

Anyone facing immediate danger should contact

the appropriate emergency service or qualified professional in their jurisdiction.

WeHere does not guarantee immediate human monitoring or intervention.

6. Peer Support Is Not Treatment

Information provided by another user is peer-generated content.

It is not:

diagnosis;

medical advice;

psychiatric advice;

therapy;

counselling; or

emergency treatment.

7. Privacy

Safety information may be processed where

necessary to operate safety mechanisms, enforce Platform rules, comply with law or protect people.

8. Counselling

WeHere does not currently provide a counselling section or professional counselling service in the application.

Any future counselling service will be separately reviewed and documented.

9. Reporting

Users should report content involving:

self-harm encouragement;

suicide encouragement;

imminent threats;

exploitation;

dangerous instructions; or

serious safety concerns.

10. Limitations

WeHere cannot guarantee:

detection of every crisis;

immediate response;

accurate location information;

emergency-service availability;

successful contact with a user; or

prevention of all harmful interactions.

INTERNAL SAFETY & MODERATION STANDARD

CONFIDENTIAL — INTERNAL USE

1. Purpose

This document establishes an internal framework for moderation and safety handling.

2. Level 1 — Ordinary Distress

Examples include loneliness, sadness, relationship problems or difficult days.

Action: Normal community moderation.

3. Level 2 — Significant Distress

Statements indicating severe hopelessness or inability to cope.

Action: Review context, monitor where appropriate and apply available safety response.

4. Level 3 — Self-Harm Ideation

Statements indicating thoughts of self-harm.

Action: Prioritise safety, review context, restrict harmful replies, provide appropriate safety information and escalate internally according to procedure.

5. Level 4 — Imminent Threat

Credible indications of immediate serious harm.

Action: Immediate safety escalation, preservation of relevant information where lawful, consideration of emergency-authority contact where legally appropriate, and restriction of harmful interactions.

6. Prohibited Responses

Moderators should not:

diagnose;

claim to be medical professionals;

promise emergency intervention;

shame users;

provide dangerous medical instructions; or

promise absolute confidentiality.

7. Documentation

Serious incidents should be documented with:

date/time;

relevant account;

relevant content;

action taken;

escalation; and

outcome where known.

8. Confidentiality

Safety records should only be accessible to personnel with a legitimate operational need.

9. Training

Relevant moderation personnel should receive training concerning:

Community Guidelines;

self-harm safety;

privacy;

harassment;

child protection;

escalation; and

documentation.

10. Review

This internal Standard should be reviewed periodically by management and legal counsel.
