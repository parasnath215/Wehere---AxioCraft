WEHERE PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: 10th October 2026

Last Updated: 7th October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how WeHere Networks Private Limited ("WeHere", "Company", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, processes, stores, protects and discloses information in connection with the WeHere mobile application, website and related services (collectively, the "Platform").

WeHere is a peer-support and social connection platform intended to help adults connect with other adults who may have similar experiences, interests, life challenges or circumstances.

WeHere is not a hospital, clinic, psychiatric service, medical service or emergency response service. The Platform does not diagnose users or provide medical treatment through its peer-support functionality.

By creating an account or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Eligibility

WeHere is intended only for individuals who are 18 years of age or older.

By creating or using an account, you represent that you are at least 18 years old.

WeHere may take reasonable measures to restrict or terminate accounts where it has reason to believe that a person does not satisfy the minimum-age requirement.

Depending on your use of the Platform, WeHere may collect:

3.1 Registration Information

name;

3.2 Social Login Information

We may permit registration or login through third-party authentication services such as Google, Apple or Facebook.

When you use such a service, we may receive information that the relevant provider makes available to us according to your settings and its applicable policies.

3.4 Anonymous Profiles

We may retain account, technical, security, transaction and other information necessary to operate the Platform, prevent abuse, comply with law and protect users.

You can manage location permissions through your device settings.

may not have access to the readable content of those communications in ordinary circumstances.

WeHere is not an emergency service.
