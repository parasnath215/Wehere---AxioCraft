Community Guidelines

WEHERE COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Effective Date: 10th October 2026

Last Updated: 8th October 2026

1. Our Community

WeHere is designed for adults to connect, communicate and provide peer support.

We want users to feel comfortable discussing personal experiences while maintaining a safe environment.

2. Respect Other Users

You must not:

bully;

harass;

threaten;

stalk;

intimidate;

repeatedly contact someone after they ask you to stop;

humiliate or degrade another user;

encourage others to target someone; or

engage in abusive conduct.

3. Hate and Discrimination

Content promoting serious hatred, violence or discrimination against protected groups is prohibited.

4. Self-Harm and Suicide

Users may discuss distress and seek support.

However, users must not:

encourage suicide;

encourage self-harm;

provide instructions intended to facilitate self-harm;

glorify suicide;

pressure another user to self-harm;

organise self-harm challenges;

deliberately target vulnerable users; or

threaten self-harm to manipulate another user.

5. Sexual Conduct

WeHere is not a dating or sexual-services platform.

Users must not:

sexually harass others;

solicit sexual services;

exploit vulnerable users;

distribute prohibited sexual content;

pressure users for sexual activity; or

use WeHere for sexual exploitation.

6. Violence

Users must not:

threaten violence;

encourage attacks;

coordinate serious violent activity; or

threaten another person with serious physical harm.

7. Fraud and Scams

Prohibited activity includes:

financial scams;

romance scams;

phishing;

impersonation;

fraudulent investment offers;

fake fundraising;

payment fraud; and

deceptive commercial activity.

8. Personal Information

Do not publish another person's private information without lawful authority.

This includes:

home addresses;

private phone numbers;

passwords;

financial information;

government identification;

private photographs; and

private communications.

9. Impersonation

Do not falsely represent yourself as another person, WeHere employee, professional or organisation.

10. Professional Misrepresentation

Users must not falsely claim to be doctors, therapists, psychologists, counsellors, lawyers or other professionals for the purpose of misleading or exploiting users.

11. Spam

Do not use WeHere for:

mass unsolicited messages;

automated spam;

fake engagement;

repetitive advertising;

malicious links; or

account manipulation.

12. Illegal Activity

The Platform must not be used to facilitate

unlawful activity.

13. User Reporting

Users can report suspected violations using available reporting functionality.

False or malicious reports may result in enforcement action.

14. Blocking

Users may block other users using available functionality.

15. Enforcement

WeHere may:

remove content;

limit visibility;

issue warnings;

restrict functionality;

suspend accounts; or

permanently terminate accounts.

Serious violations may result in immediate action.

16. Appeals

Where an appeal process is available, users may request review of an enforcement decision.

17. Safety

WeHere cannot guarantee that every harmful interaction will be identified or prevented.

Users should use appropriate caution when interacting with strangers.

18. Updates

These Guidelines may change as WeHere, applicable laws and community safety

requirements evolve.
